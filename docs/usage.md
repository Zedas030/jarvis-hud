# Usage

Speaking, typing, cards, personality, everyday life and access from iPhone, iPad and phone.

## Usage

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Live mode | Click **Live**. The button shows the state: *Listening*, *Hearing you*, *Thinking*, *Speaking*, *Microphone blocked*. With the wake word: *Say "Jarvis"*, after "Jarvis" or after every answer briefly *I am listening …*. |
| Timers and alarms | Countdown at the top of the bar. It rings, then he speaks. Talking over it, `Esc` or **Stop** ends the ringing, *"Snooze"* gives 9 minutes. |
| Notices | Appear with a yellow border in the conversation and are read out; during quiet hours they are only shown. |
| Push-to-talk | Hold the Live button, speak, let go. |
| Interrupting | Just talk over him: after 60 ms of voice he gets quieter, after 150 ms he stops and listens (Sonos: ≈ 1 s because of its buffer). If you say something, that is the new question; "Stop" ends the answer. If you say nothing, only "mhm", or the mic just hears him from the Sonos, he carries on with the sentence he started after 1.5 s. While TV or sport is on, a new question only counts with "Jarvis". Also `Esc` or the space bar. Details: [Voice engine](voice.md#voice-engine-listening-interrupting-resuming) |
| Mini window | When you leave the Jarvis tab, a small robot panda sits at the bottom right of the desktop above all windows, with a bar below it showing "Speaking / Listening / Thinking", the last line, **■ Stop** and **🎙 Mic**. The panda acts out the state (mouth when speaking, ears up when listening, head tilted when thinking, asleep when paused) and pulls a face to match the answer: from the tone tag (`[dry]` → critical, `[amused]` → happy, `[sighs]` → worried …) or from words like "unfortunately", "error", "done". His eyes follow the mouse, he is pleased about a click (five quick clicks: a pirouette) and can be moved by dragging. Back in the tab he disappears. It is a window of the server, not a browser picture-in-picture; `JARVIS_OVERLAY=0` switches it off, `JARVIS_OVERLAY_STYLE=card` brings back the old card with the orb |
| 3D printer | *"How far along is the printer?"*, *"Which filament is in the AMS?"*, *"How hot is the nozzle?"* — locally from Home Assistant (integration [ha-bambulab](https://github.com/greghesp/ha-bambulab)), 0 tokens. Jarvis lets you know by himself when a print is finished, fails, stops for a reason (filament run out, first layer, filament tangle …) or the printer reports an error. He only reads; he never controls the printer. `JARVIS_PRINTER=0` switches it off |
| Updates | *"Are there any updates?"* checks the FRITZ!Box and other Home Assistant devices, the NAS (DSM and packages) and the Pi (packages, Pi-hole). *"Install the Pi-hole update"* — every device only with an approval card, never automatically. DSM itself and Docker stay manual work; the Fire TV updates itself in standby |
| Outages | *"What happens if the Pi crashes?"*, *"What fails if the FRITZ!Box goes down?"*, *"What depends on the NAS?"* — answered at once and without Claude from the dependency map Blast-Radius on the Pi (:8098): which functions stop working and which are degraded. `BLAST_RADIUS_URL` only if Blast-Radius does not run on the Pi |
| Pi tools | *"Are the backups fine?"* (Restore Probe), *"What does the update airlock say?"*, *"Anything unusual about the power?"* (power hog detective), *"Where should I put a Zigbee router?"* (placement advisor), *"What do the tools on the Pi say?"* — at once and without Claude. The morning briefing only mentions what needs attention (failed backup test, held update, open power finding), not routine updates. *"What are my power hogs?"* stays the standby list. Own URLs: `JARVIS_RESTORE_PROBE_URL`, `JARVIS_UPDATE_AIRLOCK_URL`, `JARVIS_STROMFRESSER_URL`, `JARVIS_ZIGBEE_ADVISOR_URL` |
| Homelab | *"How fast is the internet?"*, *"How much has the Pi-hole blocked?"*, *"When was the CPU highest today?"* — live values and histories from Prometheus (FRITZ!Box, Pi, PC, Home Assistant; the same data as in Grafana) and from Pi-hole. The "Homelab" tile in the HUD. Pi-hole needs an app password in `PIHOLE_PASSWORD`; `PROMETHEUS_URL` and `PIHOLE_URL` only if they are not on the Pi |
| Live bar | Below the header: CPU, GPU, VRAM, GPU temperature, RAM and network ↓/↑ of **this PC**, every 2 seconds, with the history of the last 2 minutes. Without an NVIDIA card the GPU fields are left out |
| Restart | **⟳ Restart** at the top of the HUD restarts the server (with a question, only on the PC) and reads `.env` again — e.g. after a new password |
| Looking things up | If Jarvis does not know something and it is neither in the notes nor in his tools, he searches the web instead of saying "I don't know". Private data never goes into the search. `JARVIS_WEB_ALWAYS=0` limits that to sentences with "search", "google", "find out" … |
| Parcels | Shipping mails from all accounts (shop, carrier, tracking number) become an **all-day calendar entry** "📦 Shop · DHL" on the expected delivery day. The day is best supplied by the **carriers' notification mails** — free, without a third-party service: register your mail address once with DHL (Post & DHL app, parcel notification), DPD (myDPD/Predict), Hermes, GLS (FlexDelivery) and UPS (My Choice). Jarvis understands "tomorrow", "Monday, 26 October", "on Wednesday" and time windows ("between 10:15 and 11:15"), in English and German mails, and matches the notification to the order through the tracking number. **Amazon** works without signing in to Amazon: the mails "Ordered" (with the delivery day), "Out for delivery" (with a time window), "Delivery attempted" and "Delivered" become one shipment "📦 Amazon: item" through the order number; Prime Video and account mails stay out. Optional: `TRACK17_API_KEY` (17TRACK, all carriers) or `DHL_API_KEY`. If there already is a similar entry in the calendar on the delivery day (entered by yourself, say), he does not create a second one. If the day changes, the entry moves along; after delivery it is called "✓ delivered". In the morning of the delivery day and on "ready for pickup" Jarvis lets you know. *"Where is my parcel?"* · *"Undo the appointments"*. Same-day delivery services (Uber Eats, Flink, …) stay out |
| Several mailboxes | More accounts as `MAIL2_IMAP_HOST`, `MAIL2_USER`, `MAIL2_PASSWORD`, `MAIL2_NAME` (up to `MAIL9_`). Inbox, counter, briefing, appointments and parcels apply to all of them; an account that does not answer right now does not hold up the others. Only mails from the last **24 hours** are read (`JARVIS_MAIL_LOOKBACK_HOURS`) — inbox, appointments and parcels never look further back |
| Appointments from mails | *"Put the bookings from my mails into the calendar"* — Jarvis reads the calendar attachments of the confirmations from the last 24 hours (PC CADDIE, golftimer, Golf House …), leaves out what is past, cancelled or already entered (same day, similar title — also if you entered it yourself), names overlaps with other appointments and enters the future ones after **one** collective card in `ICLOUD_DEFAULT_CALENDAR`. Every 30 minutes (7–22 h) he looks by himself: bookings from **trusted senders** (`JARVIS_MAIL_EVENTS_TRUSTED`, default PC CADDIE, golftimer, Golf House) he enters right away and lets you know — *"Undo the appointments"* takes them out again; for all others he names the sender and the appointment explicitly and that the sender is not on the trusted list, and only suggests them. `JARVIS_MAIL_EVENTS_DAILY=0` switches that off |
| Confirming | Click the card or say "confirmed" / "no". A plain "yes" deliberately does not count. |
| Typing | Input field, `Enter` |
| Commands | `/new`, `/status`, `/recall <term>`, `/graph`, `/help` |
| API credit | ✎ in the usage field: enter the balance, spending is deducted through the Cost API |

## Control, personality and everyday life

Six build-out stages, each with its own tests. The rules behind them are in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md), the structure in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

**Trust and security**
- Every tool has a fixed class in code (`policy.py`): read, small change, only with a card, blocked. Jarvis never switches locks, alarm systems or cameras.
- Mails, web pages, calendar, files, clipboard and screen count as **third-party data**: they are marked, and after that every change in the same request needs a card.
- *"What did you do today?"* reads the audit log (`logs/audit.jsonl`).
- *"Jarvis, stop everything"* / *"Emergency stop"*: open cards are declined, Claude is stopped, notices and scheduled things pause for two hours. Alarms keep ringing. *"Carry on"* lifts it.
- Memory: every new entry carries its source and date (*said* or *inferred*). Plus *"What do you know about me?"*, *"Why did you store that?"*, *"Correct: …"*, *"Forget the thing about …"* (with a card, goes to the trash) and *"Export my memory"*. A daily backup lies in `backups/`.
- When switching, Jarvis reads the state back and says honestly when a device does not react.

**Personality**
- *"Professional mode"*, *"be casual"*, *"technical mode for two hours"*, *"back to normal"*, *"which mode?"*: local, no tokens. Own profiles in `config/profiles.json`.
- In every profile: no jokes about outages and bad news, uncertainty is said openly.
- **Typed** questions get detailed answers with lists; only the first paragraph is read out.

**Windows and screen**
- *"Spotify to the right"*, *"Browser left and Code right on monitor 2"*: local. A workspace is a routine with steps like *"Open Discord"* and *"Discord to the right"*.
- *"Find the packing list"*, *"Read the file to me"*: only in Desktop, Documents and Downloads (`JARVIS_FILE_ROOTS`). Text, code and Word, no work files.
- *"What did I copy?"*, *"What does the error message on my screen say?"*: the text recognition is local, the picture does not leave the PC. With a visible password manager, banking or messenger Jarvis refuses.
- Scripts only from `config/scripts.json`, always with a card.

**Infrastructure and smart home**
- *"How is the PC doing?"* shows CPU, RAM, GPU, VRAM (with the programs behind them) and disks. *"Why is the internet slow?"* checks router, internet, DNS and web pages and says where the problem is.
- *"Are the backups running?"* queries Hyper Backup on the NAS. The Jarvis user needs read access to the tasks for that.
- *"Are all sensors online?"* reports unreachable devices, weak batteries and silent sensors.
- The homelab watcher now also watches GPU temperature and the system disk, checks the backups daily, keeps an event history and sends critical things to the phone (`JARVIS_PHONE_NOTIFY`).

**Organisation and research**
- *"Write Anna a mail asking whether Saturday still stands"* creates a **draft** in the mailbox, with a card. Nothing is ever sent.
- *"What do I need to know for tomorrow?"* gives appointments with travel time, time to leave and matching notes. Work appointments are only counted.
- *"Research thoroughly …"* gives sources with dates, names contradictions, marks assumptions and stores the result under `Research/`.
- *"Let me know when there is news about …"*: at most 5 topics and at most 2 checks a day. Jarvis only speaks up when there really is something new.
- *"Plan my move over two weeks"* creates `Projects/Move`, with a checklist and one reminder per step.

**HUD**
- Under "CLI" it says who answered last and why (e.g. *Haiku · home · simple* or *local · router, 0 tokens*).
- A waveform shows the real microphone or voice signal, no fake animation.
- 🔔 at the top right opens the notice centre: notices, homelab outages, open suggestions, emergency stop.
- **⛶ Centre** is a full-screen command centre without input (e.g. for the second monitor, also through `?view=wall`). **▭ Mini** only shows the conversation and the voice (`?view=mini`).
- Under "Systems" there is a line for this PC.

| Variable | Default | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `JARVIS_PAUSE_HOURS` | `2` | Length of the emergency stop |
| `JARVIS_AUDIT_DAYS`, `JARVIS_BACKUP_KEEP` | `90`, `14` | Keep the audit log and vault backups |
| `JARVIS_VERIFY_SECONDS` | `2.5` | How long Jarvis waits for the new device state |
| `JARVIS_FILE_ROOTS` | Desktop, Documents, Downloads | Shared folders, comma-separated |
| `JARVIS_SCREEN_BLOCK` | empty | More window titles for which the screen is not read |
| `JARVIS_PHONE_NOTIFY`, `JARVIS_PHONE_PUSH` | empty, `critical` | HA notify service for the phone, what is pushed (`critical`, `all`, `off`) |
| `JARVIS_WATCH_MAX`, `JARVIS_WATCH_PER_DAY` | `5`, `2` | Watched topics and checks per day |
| `JARVIS_PLAN_REMIND` | `09:00` | Time of the reminders in plans |
| `config/profiles.json`, `config/scripts.json`, `config/homelab.json` | — | Own profiles, approved scripts, important devices (templates `*.example.json`) |

## On the go: iPhone and iPad

The PC stays the brain. iPhone and iPad connect through [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) (a private VPN, free for personal use) over HTTPS, from anywhere and without an open port in the router.

**Setting up once**

1. Install Tailscale on the PC and sign in, then the Tailscale app on iPhone/iPad with the same account.
2. In the Tailscale admin console under *DNS* switch on "MagicDNS" and "HTTPS Certificates".
3. In `.env`: `JARVIS_REMOTE=1` and restart Jarvis. The access for devices then runs on `127.0.0.1:8721` (`JARVIS_REMOTE_PORT`).
4. Once on the PC: `tailscale serve --bg 8721`. Tailscale shows the address, e.g. `https://my-pc.tailXXXX.ts.net`.

**Pairing a device**

1. Open the address in Safari on the iPhone or iPad, *Share → Add to Home Screen* (iPhone: Share at the bottom, iPad: top right).
2. Open Jarvis from the Home Screen (the app has its own sign-in, separate from Safari).
3. On the PC in the HUD go to **📱 Devices → New code** and enter the six-digit code on the device. It is valid for five minutes and only once.

**📱 Devices** lists all paired devices; ✕ locks one out right away.

**On the phone:** tapping the microphone button switches live on and off, holding it is push-to-talk. The microphone never starts by itself, there is no daily briefing, and notices appear silently (the PC reads them out). The wake word only listens on the PC.

**On the iPad:** a view of its own instead of the command centre, like a calm room display. On the left a big clock with a greeting, the weather now and for five days and the next appointments of the week. On the right a sphere glows that shows what Jarvis is doing right now: waiting for "Jarvis", listening, thinking, speaking. Everything fits on one screen without scrolling: the mic button, quick commands and a row of tiles are always visible; more appointments scroll in their box. Jarvis' answers are not shown as text here, he speaks them; only an approval card appears below the sphere so that you can tap it. Below that your question and his answer are shown large, plus the mic button, ⌨ for typing and a few quick commands. At the bottom tiles show solar, battery, rooms, air, mail, sleep and tech (NAS, Pi, internet). Tapping a tile or the weather opens a sheet with all the details: single batteries, target temperatures, the newest mails, Garmin values, the state of the devices. For the weather each day says right away when it rains, how likely and how much (e.g. "rain 6–12 h (30–100 %), 23–24 h (18 %) · ~1.8 mm"). Every hour from 30 % counts as a rain hour, with a forecast amount already from 10 %, and the preview shows the times under each day. The sheet shows the same line for today and the next four days, and below it the 24 hours as bars. If not everything fits on the screen, the page can be scrolled. The ⋯ menu holds the station, restart and the way back to the classic view with the memory graph. **Layout per device:** in the classic view next to the name JARVIS, in the room display under ⋯, a selection field picks the layout for exactly this device: *Room display*, *Stark HUD* or *Classic with memory graph*. That way the iPad by the bed gets a calm display, a wall monitor the workshop look and the desk PC the command centre. As long as a device has not chosen anything, it shows *Sun path · day & moon* — that is the default everywhere, and the choice stored on the server takes precedence over anything the page kept locally. For a look, `?view=tablet`, `?view=stark` or `?view=classic` works too (not from inside a Home Screen app, where the stored choice counts).

**Stark HUD:** the same parts as the room display (mic, answers, approval cards, tiles, detail sheets) in the Iron Man style: black glass, cyan, chamfered corners, a grid and a honeycomb field. At the top a day strip of the month, on the left the clock with seconds, weather and appointments, in the middle an arc reactor as the avatar, on the right the engine room as instruments: CPU, RAM, VRAM and disk of the PC as rings, network, Pi-hole, energy and the state of the NAS, containers and internet. The reactor glows with his voice; when listening it turns gold and brackets close, when thinking a light runs through the coils, with problems it turns red. In portrait the reactor is at the top with the day and the systems side by side below it; the areas are stacked. On the PC the view can be seen with `?view=tablet`. Unlike on the phone, the microphone is on at every start and listens for "Jarvis", also after a restart. Switching it off only lasts until the next load. If you come back to the app after more than two seconds, it reloads, because iOS takes the microphone away from it in the background. If iOS wants a tap once, the whole screen shows "Tap so that Jarvis listens". Briefing and notices behave as on the phone.

**iPad as a station (screen and microphone):** for an iPad that stands in a fixed place. Tap **🎙 Station**: the iPad shows the command centre, the screen stays on, and from now on the microphone listens for "Jarvis …". The answer comes from the iPad. The setting only applies to this device and survives a restart. If the page reloads (e.g. after a Jarvis restart), iOS sometimes wants a tap once. Then the screen shows "Tap so that Jarvis listens". Notices stay silent; the PC reads them out. If the PC microphone and the iPad hear the same sentence, the room decides: `config/home.json` → `mic_rooms` names, per room of the presence sensors, the device whose microphone counts (`{"Bedroom": "iPad"}`, the name from pairing). If I am in the bedroom, the iPad takes the sentence; the PC waits briefly after its end of sentence (`JARVIS_MIC_HOLD_S`, 1.5 s) to see whether the iPad finished the same sentence (`JARVIS_MIC_SAME_S`, 3 s), and then drops it ("the iPad is listening" in the log). If only the PC hears something, for example because the iPad is locked, it answers after the waiting time; in other rooms and without a connected iPad everything stays as before.

**Answer through the Sonos:** selectable per device, where Jarvis answers. On the iPad under ⋯ (on the PC in the toolbar) **🔈 Sound here** switches to **🔊 Sonos bedroom** and back. Then every answer to a question from this device, typed or spoken, comes from the Sonos as an announcement. As with Alexa, music on the Sonos goes down to `JARVIS_SONOS_QUIET` (default 12 %) already on "Jarvis" or when sending, whether it plays through Spotify, AirPlay or the Sonos app. After the answer the old volume comes back, unless someone has turned the knob in the meantime. The Sonos already starts with the first finished sentence (about 1–2 seconds after it); all further sentences run into the same announcement, and silence bridges gaps. So the music does not come up between the sentences. The speakers are in `JARVIS_SONOS` (`media_player.x=Name`, several with commas), the volume in `JARVIS_SONOS_VOLUME` and during quiet hours in `JARVIS_SONOS_NIGHT_VOLUME`. The Sonos fetches the clip itself from the PC through a second small server in the home network (`JARVIS_SONOS_PORT`, default 8723). If the announcement does not work, the device speaks itself. Notices by himself are still read out by the PC.

**Jarvis knows which room I am in:** three presence sensors (SONOFF SNZB-06P24, Zigbee, 24 GHz) in the bathroom, bedroom and living room report to Home Assistant. There the automation *"Jarvis: where is the user"* sets the helper `input_select.user_room` to **Living room**, **Bedroom** (bathroom and bedroom count as one room, they are next to each other) or **Unknown**. The sensor that fired last wins. If no sensor sees anyone (sleep, gaming room), the last room stays; it only goes to Unknown when `person.user` is not at home. Jarvis reads the helper (`config/home.json` → `presence_room`, at most every 10 s, `JARVIS_PRESENCE_TTL_S`) and: (1) the output button has another position **🔊 Where I am** — the answer comes from the speaker of the room I am in right now (Echo in the living room, Sonos in the bedroom), and from the device itself when no sensor sees me (for example at the PC in the gaming room). A device that has never chosen anything is set to it automatically; **🔈 Sound here** stays as a deliberate choice. (2) The model gets the whereabouts in the `<context>`; "Announce: …" without a room and "bring the music to me" go to that room. (3) Without Jarvis and without the PC: the HA automation *"Jarvis: music follows me"* takes the music along — if something plays in the old room and I change rooms, it goes to the speaker there through Spotify Connect (HA Spotify integration `media_player.spotify_user`, `select_source`; seamless, same position). In the living room the target is the Spotify device "Living room", which is the soundbar (`config/home.json` → `speakers."Living room".spotify`); if it is missing from Spotify's device list, it is tried three times a few seconds apart (`JARVIS_MUSIC_ROOM_TRIES`, `JARVIS_MUSIC_ROOM_PAUSE_S`), and only then does the weaker Echo Dot step in alone (`spotify_fallback`); the HA automation does the same. Never the Echo through Alexa commands. (4) *"Jarvis: birds in the bathroom"*: when the bathroom sensor fires and no music is playing, the Sonos in the bedroom next door plays birdsong in a loop (local file `www/jarvis/birds.ogg` in the HA configuration, 7 minutes of "Birdsong morning 01" by Matutinho, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0); before that a Sonos snapshot, after the bathroom a restore, so back to the state before. If music is playing, the music wins; at night (23–6 h) the birds stay off. (5) All these automations only switch when `person.user` is at home — the sensors alone do nothing; if one fires while the phone has been away for 10 minutes, a push message comes to the phone instead (*"Jarvis: movement at home without the phone"*). (6) *"Jarvis: good night"*: between 22 and 3 h, the living room empty for 10 minutes and me in the bedroom or bathroom → living room LED off, the Sonos briefly says good night; the TV socket stays on because the soundbar hangs on it. In the morning the first trip to the bathroom triggers the morning briefing, spoken in the room I am in. If I am not at home in the evening, nothing switches on — a presence simulation with the LED existed briefly and was removed again on request. (7) *"Jarvis: heating follows me"*: only living room and bedroom, the gaming room stays untouched. If I am in one of the two rooms and its room sensor shows below 17 °C, the Kasa valves there switch on — always at 20 °C, my default; a temperature changed through Jarvis applies until the next switch-on. If I have not been in the room for 20 minutes, its valves switch off; if the phone has not been at home for 15 minutes, both rooms switch off. Bedroom fixed: off daily at 23 h (no reheating until 6 h), on at 6 h with 20 °C if I am at home; on weekdays I am usually out of the house by 7:30, then "phone away" applies. The switch `input_boolean.jarvis_heating_follows_me` suspends the rule (summer, visitors). Off and on again costs nothing extra: the heat a room needs depends on its temperature — every hour cooler is saved, and reheating only brings back part of it. (8) HA counts the time per room (`sensor.time_living_room_7_days`, `sensor.time_bedroom_7_days`, plus today and the bathroom visits), and the weekly review gets a line from it. If the Sonos in the bedroom is not in Spotify's device list right now (only while it plays), the Alexa in the Sonos Era 100 plays the same track on through Spotify instead and the old one pauses; then the position in the playlist is lost.

**Commands without a room mean the room I am in** (`jarvis/core/room_scope.py`, switch `JARVIS_ROOM_SCOPE`, 0 = off, then everything is as before): *"light on"*, *"TV on"*, *"heating on"*, *"to 21 degrees"*, *"warmer"* / *"colder"* (1 °C each, `JARVIS_CLIMATE_STEP`; a switched-off heating goes on at 20 °C on "warmer", `JARVIS_CLIMATE_DEFAULT`) mean the room the helper `input_select.user_room` names. Which devices are in which room comes from the **areas in Home Assistant** (Settings → Areas); `config/home.json` → `room_scope.areas` puts finer areas onto a presence room (`{"Bathroom": "Bedroom"}`). A room in the sentence always wins (*"turn on the TV in the living room"*). The bedroom has no switchable light: *"light on"* there asks *"Shall I turn on the one in the Living room?"*, a *"yes"* switches it, *"no"* leaves it; *"light off"* switches off what is on without asking. **Unsure situation:** `room_scope.sensors` lists the presence sensors per room. If the sensor of the helper's room is free but another one is busy (I am walking, or a radar ghost), Jarvis asks *"Living room or Bedroom?"* – but only when the answer changes something (*"TV on"* yes, *"light off"* no). The answer (*"Bedroom"*, *"in the living room"*) runs the command there, *"forget it"* cancels. If no sensor sees anyone (asleep, sitting still at the desk), the helper's room counts. **Claude** gets the room in the `<context>`, whether it is sure, and up to eight devices there with their entity ids, so it switches the right thing without asking. **Outages:** if Home Assistant does not answer, the last room still counts for 5 minutes (`JARVIS_PRESENCE_STALE_S`), after that it is unknown; commands without a room then run as before. With "Where I am" the device itself answers in that case and says why, once per outage (*"I cannot see which room you are in right now …"*); the same when the Sonos is *unavailable* in HA or the announcement does not start (*"The speaker … does not answer …"*). **iPad:** the room display shows **Room** as its first tile – the room, *unsure · Living room or Bedroom* or *Home Assistant not reachable*. Check without switching: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\room_probe.py` (once) or `… --watch` (every 3 s, for a walk through the flat).

For the screen to stay on, iPadOS 18.4 or newer is needed. On older versions set *Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never*.

**Wake word on the device:** run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\fetch_wakeword.py` once on the PC. The script downloads onnxruntime-web (MIT) and the openWakeWord models (Apache 2.0), together about 14 MB, checks their checksums and puts them into `ui/vendor/` (not in the repo). iPad and iPhone then detect "Jarvis" themselves. A clip only goes to the PC when the device has heard something like "Jarvis". Conversations in the room, phone calls and the TV stay on the device. The model is trained on "Hey Jarvis" and detects that most reliably. A bare "Jarvis" lies just above the threshold `JARVIS_WAKE_LOCAL_THRESHOLD` (default 0.02). A clip up to 8 seconds, i.e. a normal request, only has to be above 0.004, because the model detects a bare "Jarvis" poorly (measured 0.005 to 0.07; noises without "Jarvis" are at 0). If the iPad is sure (from 0.06 for short clips), that also counts when Whisper misspells the name. The PC then still checks the text. What the detection on iPad and iPhone does (ready, dropped or to the PC, with score, threshold and length, never words) is in `logs/wake_device.log` on the PC. The threshold can be set from that. The iPad downloads the files once and keeps them. If they are missing, the PC checks everything as before.

**Only "Jarvis" instead of "Hey Jarvis":** the script also downloads two community models trained on a bare "Jarvis" ([fwartner/home-assistant-wakewords-collection](https://github.com/fwartner/home-assistant-wakewords-collection), MIT): `jarvis_v1` hears more and errs more often, `jarvis_v2` the other way round. Which model decides is in `JARVIS_WAKE_LOCAL_MODEL` (default `hey_jarvis`). The others run along on the device and are only logged: every line in `logs/wake_device.log` then shows each model's score, e.g. `hey_jarvis 0.012 · jarvis_v1 0.640 · jarvis_v2 0.210`. Talk normally for an evening, then enter the model with the clearest scores for "Jarvis" and set the threshold accordingly. The values above apply to `hey_jarvis`.

**Music, sport, TV in the background:** Jarvis notices it in two ways. Home Assistant reports media players that are playing (every 20 seconds; sport he recognises from the title, channel or app like DAZN or Sky Sport). And the microphone hears when it is loud almost without a pause for about ten seconds, because people pause. Then the detection gets stricter: "Jarvis" only counts at the start or end of a sentence, not in the middle of a commentator's sentence, and the low bar for a short "Jarvis" is dropped. The display then shows for example "sport on · say “Hey Jarvis”", because "Hey Jarvis" always gets through. `JARVIS_AMBIENCE=0` switches the Home Assistant query off, `JARVIS_AMBIENCE_IGNORE` leaves out players that can never be heard in the room. Spotify only counts when it can be heard here: Home Assistant's Spotify player stands for the whole account and is skipped (`JARVIS_AMBIENCE_SPOTIFY_ACCOUNT`); Spotify playing on a phone or tablet only counts while someone is at home (since 2026-11-05; before that, music from the iPhone on the road counted as "music playing").

**Sound without tapping:** iOS only lets a web page play sound after a touch. If the microphone is already running, Jarvis plays his voice through its audio channel instead, so nobody has to tap. The first sentence then comes about a second later, because it is loaded in full first.

When locked the iPad does not listen, because iOS switches off the microphone of a locked web app. For continuous operation therefore *Auto-Lock → Never*, best combined with *Guided Access* (Accessibility).

**Night screen:** as long as the sensor `station_night` in `config/home.json` is on, the station goes black and only shows a barely visible clock. The default is `binary_sensor.ipad_focus` from the Home Assistant app: it is on while a focus runs on the iPad, so also the sleep focus from the sleep schedule. The microphone keeps listening. During a conversation the HUD shines through dimmed, and a tap shows it for 20 seconds. A web page cannot control the backlight. For that create two shortcut automations on the iPad: *Sleep focus turns on → brightness 0 %* and *turns off → brightness 60 %*. In the sleep focus switch off the *Sleep screen*, otherwise iOS locks the iPad. The sensor comes through the query of the dashboard values, so switching takes up to a minute.

**Saving the battery with a smart socket:** the charger hangs on a Tapo socket (`switch.ipad_socket`). The Home Assistant automation *iPad station: charging between 30 and 95 %* switches it off from 95 % and on again below 30 %. The battery level is reported by two shortcut automations on the iPad (*Shortcuts → Automation → Battery Level*: "rises above 95 %" or "falls below 30 %", *Run immediately*, action *Get Contents of URL*, method POST). They call the automation's two webhooks; the URLs are in the automation in Home Assistant. Alternatively the Home Assistant app on the iPad is enough (`sensor.ipad_battery_level`). If the socket stays off for ten hours, it switches on again for safety.

### Phone: yes and no on the go

Suggestions that matter on the go (*"switch off"* when leaving, *"switch off before setting off"*) Jarvis sends to the phone with two buttons (`JARVIS_PHONE_NOTIFY`, Home Assistant app). A tap on **Yes, do it** counts like the card in the HUD: Jarvis carries out the suggestion, says so and writes it into the audit (`card`, source `phone`). The way back goes through Home Assistant, to be set up once:

1. Create *Settings → Devices & services → Helpers → Text*: `input_text.jarvis_phone_action` (or another name in `JARVIS_PHONE_ACTION_ENTITY`).
2. An automation: trigger *Event* `mobile_app_notification_action`; action *input_text.set_value* on the helper with the value `{{ trigger.event.data.action }}`.

Jarvis reads the helper every 30 seconds together with the other states, carries out `JARVIS_YES_<id>` or `JARVIS_NO_<id>` and empties it again. Only suggestions that Jarvis created himself and that are still open can be confirmed this way; every value is processed exactly once.
